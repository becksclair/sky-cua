## KWrite Computer Use

KWrite behaves a lot like the simpler edge of Kate's editing surface on KDE. Prefer
semantic reads of focused controls, values, text readback, and window state, but
treat editor-content replacement as keyboard-driven unless the specific editor
element exposes and verifies a semantic `set_value` path.

Use one click to focus the editor area before typing. Prefer full replacement flows
like click -> Ctrl+A -> type over assuming a semantic value-write contract.
